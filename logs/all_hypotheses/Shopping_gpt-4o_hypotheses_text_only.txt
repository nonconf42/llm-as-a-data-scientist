Active subscribers show a greater tendency to use promo codes. By increasing promo code accessibility for subscribed users, businesses can drive engagement and increase cumulative sales.
Young adults tend to provide lower ratings than older age groups, indicating potential dissonance in product or service satisfaction. Focusing feedback initiatives and enhancements on this demographic could improve overall satisfaction.

Promo code users tend to have higher purchase amounts, suggesting that promo code strategies can effectively increase average spend. Tailoring promo code offers could enhance revenue by encouraging higher-value purchases.
Subscribers are more engaged with the brand, purchasing more frequently than non-subscribers. Thus, subscription models could enhance customer loyalty and sales frequency, advising further development of subscriber benefits.
Applying discounts can enhance customer satisfaction, evident through improved review ratings. This suggests that periodic discounts can positively impact customer reviews and potentially increase brand reputation.
Express shipping significantly influences customer satisfaction. Enhancing rapid delivery services and prioritizing express options may improve overall customer perceptions and feedback.
Analyzing seasonal shopping preferences reveals distinct trends: heavier clothing and outerwear purchases in winter; lighter attire in summer. Marketing seasonal promotions helps target specific consumer needs effectively.
Credit card transactions usually have higher values than PayPal transactions, suggesting credit card-focused promotions might yield greater revenue increases and should be emphasized in marketing strategies.
Footwear is frequently discounted compared to other categories. Recognizing this trend can guide tactical decisions in pricing strategies and sales promotions, optimizing margins and sales performances.
